# The Silent Bell

Version 1 · Doll short

Drelli Bunt (a), a glossy adult fashion doll with folded hands, proudly explains
that a repaired bell will never make a sound. She pauses to demonstrate. The bell
stays silent. The drawer beneath the bench makes a single small click instead.

Give Drelli one short line in `promise`, then cut to `latch` with her hands
still folded. Keep `latch` free of dialogue with a 2000 ms hold. Put its one
sound cue, the pinned drawer-latch click, at 1250 ms on that board's clock.
Cut at the hold boundary. Let the pause carry the joke.
