# The Official Marble

Version 1 · Doll short

Tessip Dovell (a) is a glossy adult fashion doll with a molded frown, posted
at a miniature inspection bench. She presents one chipped teal marble as the
official unit of measurement. It is the only distinctive object on the bench.
Then it rolls out of the holder while she is still taking her job very seriously.

Write one short line for Tessip in `inspection`. Cut to a silent no-cast
`marble-insert` with the pinned marble as the photo input. Frame the chip and the
shallow velvet holder so the audience can see what is rolling away. Give the
empty holder a beat after the roll.
