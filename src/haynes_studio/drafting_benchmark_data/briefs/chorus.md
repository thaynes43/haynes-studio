# The Spare Ribbon

Version 1 · Doll short

Four adult fashion dolls from the Mothspool Relay Club sit behind a miniature
awards table. Their patch is three crooked stitches on violet felt. Brix Tovel
(a), Marn Quillip (b), Nessa Borlo (c) and Dex Pellock (d) each name the event they
won. The events get more administrative with each turn. Everyone is trying to
claim the club's single award ribbon.

Use `roll-call` to place all four molded faces in the same shot. Give each doll
one short turn, in slot order, delivered like a serious athletic result. Cut to
`clothespin` for a silent final reaction when Brix hangs the ribbon on an ordinary
clothespin and treats it as the overall winner. Keep each doll's expression fixed
within its shot.
