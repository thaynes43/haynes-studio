# A Queue for the Queue

Version 1 · Doll short

Sibbet Vosko (a) runs a toy queue kiosk. Arlo Pindel (b) discovers
he needs a ticket to join the queue that hands out tickets. Both are adult
fashion dolls with rigid painted stares. Each responds as if the other has
missed a trivial procedure.

Revise only `exchange`. Place both dolls in that board and write six short,
alternating turns, starting with Sibbet. Use the turns to lead to Arlo quietly
pointing out that he was already first in the second queue. Give every line a
clear handoff to the next. Leave the person-edited `notice` and the unstamped
`ticket-button` board exactly as supplied, in their original positions.
