## Premise

A girls' soccer team of fashion dolls has been stranded in the woods for almost a
year. Wearing their matching team jackets, now well worn, they calmly discuss who to
eat for the winter. One cheerfully suggests they just eat their crunchy butter
squishies instead. The others turn to look at her, and the group decides to eat her.
It ends on deadpan silence. No violence is shown.

Parody source: the stranded-soccer-team survival premise from popular TV. Everything
on screen is our own: the team name, the jacket design and the dialogue. Do not use
the show's name, logo, characters or lines.

The butter squishies tie back to "Crunch Costs Extra" (OP345).

## Set

A 1:6-scale campsite in a frosty pine forest (Tom, 2026-11-03: "have them around a camp
fire ... like a campsite"): a small stone fire ring with a lit campfire, a stick
lean-to and a tiny camping pot behind it, frost and pine needles on the ground, and a
mossy log across the bottom of frame as the foreground strip (the bottom 22% of the
frame, matching the editor). Soft overcast light with warm firelight, shallow depth
of field, the middle of the frame clear for the dolls.
