Stop-motion style toy video. The same the plastic doll with feral ash-blonde hair, a twig crown and moss, in the same blue YELLOWBIRDS team jacket, stands behind a mossy log at a miniature campsite. An unseen child plays with her: the whole doll bobs and rocks as if making a suggestion, her frizzy hair shifts. Their faces are hard molded plastic: painted eyes and expressions never change and mouths do not move. Static locked-off camera, same framing, soft overcast light, miniature toy photography. The small campfire on the left flickers and its flames dance, thin smoke rises. Pine branches sway slightly.
