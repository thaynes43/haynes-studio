Stop-motion style toy video. The same the plastic doll with platinum pigtails, a pink bow, holding a yellow butter squishy, in the same sleeveless blue YELLOWBIRDS jacket, stands behind a mossy log at a miniature campsite. An unseen child plays with her: the whole doll jerks back and shakes in protest, clutching the squishy, her pigtails swing. Their faces are hard molded plastic: painted eyes and expressions never change and mouths do not move. Static locked-off camera, same framing, soft overcast light, miniature toy photography. The small campfire on the left flickers and its flames dance, thin smoke rises. Pine branches sway slightly.
