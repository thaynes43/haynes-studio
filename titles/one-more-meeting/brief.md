# One More Meeting

A committee chair asks whether anyone objects to cancelling the next meeting. The silence lasts a beat. She takes it as a reason to schedule another one.

One locked vertical shot at the stock miniature campsite. Use the existing chair doll with folded hands and Kokoro stock voice `af_heart`. The chair holds her deadpan expression through the pause and gives the second line the same matter-of-fact delivery as the first. No other speakers or sound cues.

> Any objections to cancelling the next meeting?
