# The Office of Lost Tuesdays

Pim Rattle tries to claim a missing Tuesday at a municipal lost-property desk.
The clerk wants a description and a receipt. The joke is that this office treats
time like a misplaced umbrella.

- **Arrival** — Pim names the thing she lost.
- **Paperwork** — She pushes a very specific receipt across the counter.
- **Aftermath** — The spare-day drawer opens just far enough to reveal a stack
  of identical slips.
